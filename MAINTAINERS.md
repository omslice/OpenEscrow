## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are selected through sustained, reviewed participation rather than a single pull
request, employment, sponsorship, donation, token holding, or organizational affiliation. The
criteria and appointment/removal process are defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

The next maintainership priority is an independent contributor capable of reviewing the contract,
application-security, release, and privacy boundaries. A narrower documentation, accessibility,
compliance-source, or community-maintainer scope may be granted when that better matches the
contributor's demonstrated work.

## Authority boundary

Repository access does not automatically grant control of wallets, deployments, domains, hosted
data, private evidence, fiscal-sponsor accounts, funds, legal decisions, or production releases.
Those systems require separate least-privileged authorization and owner-controlled gates.

For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For participation expectations, follow
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
