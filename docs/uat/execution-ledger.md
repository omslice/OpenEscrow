## Retest and adjacent-regression rule

A defect may move to `Closed` only when:

1. the original scenario passes on the fixed build;
2. focused automated coverage fails before the fix or otherwise proves the defect boundary;
3. adjacent role, value, notification, record, and recovery scenarios pass; and
4. the hosted or local environment appropriate to the defect is recorded.
