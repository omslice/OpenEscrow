## Registration source distinction

The [Lucas County Auditor](https://www.lucascountyohioauditor.gov/rentalreg) says residential rental
properties must be registered and provides an AREIS rental-registration check. The Ottawa Hills
intake should request confirmation of current registration or a supported exception where applicable.
Keep actual addresses, owner details and registration records in the private participant record.

[R.C. 5323.01](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5323.01), effective September 29,
2007, defines covered residential rental property using a county population greater than 200,000
in the most recent decennial census. [R.C. 5323.02](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-5323.02),
effective September 30, 2025, specifies owner/entity contact and parcel information and updates
within 60 days of changed information.

The [Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/portagecountyohio/PST040225)
reports Portage County's **2020 decennial population as 161,791**. The engineering inference is
that the Chapter 5323 county-population definition does not cover Portage on this evidence.
This does **not** establish that no other local registration or inspection requirement exists.
Do not copy Lucas County's intake requirement into a Portage policy without its own authority.

  [code publisher](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/ottawahills/latest/overview) labels the

  [legislation records](https://ottawahills.org/residents/legislation-and-minutes) and obtain
  confirmation of later amendments before treating the compilation as complete.
- Franklin Township's [zoning page](https://www.franklintownshipohio.org/zoning-team) links a
  [318-page resolution updated through April 28, 2026](https://www.franklintownshipohio.org/_files/ugd/008776_b8e99a5e39404d5fb09edba83e794620.pdf).
  Its contents include distinct Brady Lake residential districts in sections 311, 314 and 316.
  Do not assume every Brady Lake parcel falls in section 314. The
  [township FAQ](https://www.franklintownshipohio.org/zoning-faq) directs readers to locate the
  parcel on the zoning map and says changes of use require a zoning certificate. Verify the
  actual tenancy/use with the current district text and any later amendments. The zoning page
  also identifies an exterior property-maintenance code; obtain the operative version.
- No complete, current local deposit overlay or negative finding on rental licensing has been
  established for either candidate. Source discovery is not review approval. Do not enable a
  new local legal profile or represent state-only routing as complete local coverage.
