# Funding transparency and continuity execution checklist

Reviewed September 14, 2026. OpenEscrow is currently documented as founder-maintained.
Existing private organizational-choice, funding, pilot-support and name-protection preparation
packets should be reused. Their drafts are not proof of a formed entity, received funding,
signed partnership, trademark clearance, or an accepted outside role.

## Funding opening facts

The published funding configuration still has `openingBalanceConfirmed: false`, no confirmed
recipient/contact, and no approved ledger entries. This means **unconfirmed**, not zero funding.

Before populating the public ledger, obtain and privately reconcile:

- [ ] Owner confirmation of all received grants, donations, sponsorships, rewards, investment,
  returned funds, expenses and in-kind support through a stated date.
- [ ] Recipient's accurate legal/project description and monitored funding contact.
- [ ] For every entry: date, currency, amount, status, restrictions, purpose and supporting receipt.
- [ ] Distinguish applications, commitments, actual receipts, expenditures and in-kind support.
- [ ] Public attribution/amount/evidence permission; redact private correspondence and identifiers.
- [ ] Opening-balance reconciliation and explicit owner confirmation of the publishable facts.

The donation ENS label is not proof of the recipient's legal identity, wallet control, charitable
status, tax deductibility, or an opening balance. Verify any receiving-address disclosure before
representing those facts publicly.

## Organization and ownership decisions

| Decision | Current evidence | Next concrete deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Nonprofit, fiscal sponsorship, for-profit, or undecided | Prior decision packet leaves the choice open | Owner selects a provisional direction; counsel reviews the operating and IP implications |
| Pilot relationship | Preparation exists; a signed agreement is not verified here | Owner supplies signed evidence and attribution permission if obtained |
| Trademarks and project assets | Preparation does not establish clearance or a transfer | Record ownership and obtain advice before filing or transfer |
| Funding programs | Historical application packets exist | Revalidate current program status and facts before reuse; do not treat an old deadline as current |

## Continuity roles

For each role, record a primary owner, a consenting backup, least-privilege access, recovery
location, a tested handoff date and review cadence. Keep credentials and recovery material private.

| Role | Responsibility | Acceptance evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Independent technical maintainer | Review, release checks and repository continuity | Named, consenting maintainer and protected access |
| Deployment/domain backup | DNS, hosting, build and account recovery | Access/recovery drill with private records |
| Security/privacy operator | Intake, triage, retention/legal holds and escalation | Receive/reply tests, backup cadence and incident drill |
| Funding record owner | Reconciliation, restrictions and public reporting | Confirmed opening facts and recurring review responsibility |
| Renter/community adviser | Participant interests and comprehension | Consented scoped advisory role |
| Housing/dispute adviser | Pilot process and escalation | Consented scoped role; legal advice only within qualification |
| Security/open-source adviser | Independent technical and stewardship input | Consented scoped role and conflicts disclosure |

Use [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) for appointments, conflicts and succession. The current
maintainer remains responsible until another person actually accepts a role. Preparing this
checklist does not authorize external invitations, signatures, incorporation, expenditure or
changes to account ownership.
