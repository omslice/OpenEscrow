## Remediation categories

- Changed: compare the reviewed document with current official text; distinguish substantive changes from page/header changes. Do not reset a baseline merely because the current fetch works.
- Unreachable: repair or replace the official source/transport after validating the full document and version. Retain review/freshness gates.
- Unchanged: verify age, exact profile/source identity and observation method; HTTP success alone does not imply legal review.
- Current source hashes include response metadata and a bounded body sample. A changed hash is a review signal, not proof that the law changed.
- Sources pointing to secondary official material require review of whether that material actually covers the cited rule. Ohio is corrected in the candidate; other replacements remain a separate source-review queue.
