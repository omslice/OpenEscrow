# OpenEscrow Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenEscrow is committed to a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Housing and financial workflows can involve unequal power and personal hardship. Contributors must
treat renters, landlords, housing workers, researchers, reviewers, and one another with dignity.
Disagreement about code, policy, law, risk, or product direction is welcome when it remains specific,
evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy, patience, and respect for different experiences and communication styles.
- Give and accept constructive feedback focused on the work and its effects.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and credit others' contributions.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and participant information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts and avoid using project access for personal leverage.
- Make room for people directly affected by rental-deposit systems, especially when technical
  convenience conflicts with participant safety or accessibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, intimidation, or publishing another person's private information.
- Retaliation against a reporter, reviewer, dissenter, security researcher, or community participant.
- Pressuring anyone to reveal housing status, immigration status, identity, finances, evidence,
  credentials, wallet keys, or other sensitive information.
- Misrepresenting affiliation, audit status, legal approval, adoption, funding, or another person's
  endorsement.
- Using donations, employment, vendor relationships, tokens, or status to buy technical decisions or
  favorable treatment in a dispute.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository activity, project communications, events, research sessions,
advisory work, social spaces represented as official, and private interactions that materially
affect a person's ability to participate safely in OpenEscrow.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `privacy@openescrow.io` with the subject
`[CONDUCT] OpenEscrow report`. Do not place sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Include only
the information needed to understand and respond; avoid forwarding unrelated personal data.

The current founding maintainer is responsible for enforcement. That single-person process is a
known governance limitation and will be replaced with an independent escalation path when another
maintainer or formal advisory/governance body is actually seated. If a report concerns the founding
maintainer, state that conflict in the subject and request an independent reviewer before sharing
additional sensitive details.

Possible responses include a private clarification, warning, content removal, participation limits,
temporary suspension, permanent removal, access revocation, or referral to a platform, fiscal
sponsor, employer, event organizer, or appropriate authority. Responses should be proportionate,
documented privately, attentive to retaliation risk, and disclose no more than safety requires.

Anyone enforcing this policy must protect reporter confidentiality as far as reasonably possible,
identify conflicts, and recuse when an independent decision maker is available. A person affected by
an enforcement decision may request reconsideration with new context or evidence.
